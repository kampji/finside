Keep your look

Version 0.9.1 · November 5, 2026

  • Upgrading keeps your scape the way it was: the same spacing, centered in the bigger tank, with open water on both sides to grow into. Tap Preview next to an upgrade to see your tank at the next size before you pay.
  • Flip any decoration, or turn it on its side or upside down. Open a decoration and tap Flip or Turn. Tap Stand it up to put it back.
  • Shrimp now crawl into the shrimp tower’s holes now and then, and stay hidden for a bit.
  • The sunken galleon has missing planks, barnacles, a rope, a lantern that glows at night, and a treasure chest. The temple ruins, rock ridge and rollercoaster got more detail too.
  • Tank lights switched on at night are brighter, so the tank doesn’t look gloomy.
  • Turn your phone sideways to see the whole tank zoomed out. Pinch to zoom in. (Before, turning it sideways blew everything up.)
  • A simpler start screen: one button to start your tank.

The 40 gallon

Version 0.9.0 · November 4, 2026

  • A 40 gallon tank! Upgrade a 20 gallon in the Tanks tab. It’s wider than the 20 and a third taller: it opens on the sand, and you can drag up to see the surface.
  • Ten new fish for the 40: angelfish, German blue rams, kribensis, Boesemani rainbowfish, pearl gouramis, marbled hatchetfish, Congo tetras, tiger barbs, bronze corydoras and Siamese algae eaters. Check the risk meter: angelfish eat neon tetras, and tiger barbs nip long fins.
  • Two new invertebrates: bamboo shrimp, which hold up little fans to catch food, and assassin snails, which hunt ramshorn and trumpet snails when they take over.
  • Six new plants for the 40: giant val, water wisteria, cabomba, aponogeton, Hygrophila Stricta and floating water lettuce.
  • Seven big decorations for the 40: a sunken rollercoaster whose train keeps riding, a slowly turning Ferris wheel, a sunken galleon, a rock ridge, a volcano bubbler, a stone arch and temple ruins.
  • Ten more animals now have a rare color to find, each one a real color form found in the fish keeping hobby.
  • Bigger fish are drawn bigger. Fish are now sized by how long they really grow, so gouramis, cichlids, plecos and the new 40 gallon fish all look larger. The smallest fish and the betta stay the same size.
  • Upgrading a tank always costs less than buying that size new now. Upgrade prices stay the same, and new 10 and 20 gallon tanks cost more than before.
  • Decorations and plants now layer by size: big ones stand at the back, so they no longer cover smaller things in front of them.
  • Decorations now take up floor space by size, so big pieces fill a tank faster. Hanging signs, the magnetic ledge and anything sitting on a ledge or rock don’t use any. Big decorations also give shy fish more cover.
  • Move a single plant or decoration straight into another tank from its card, no storage needed.
  • New achievements: Botanist for growing every kind of plant, Collector for owning every Shop decoration, and Big water for your first 40 gallon. Master aquarist, for keeping every species, now pays 40,000 coins.

Roomy storage and helping hands

Version 0.8.0 · November 4, 2026

  • Storage has 10 free slots now, and up to 20 of the same plant or decoration share one slot. Any slots you bought are still yours, on top. Tap a slot, then Place in… to choose which tank it goes to, or tap Select to move several things at once. Nothing has to wait in storage anymore: whatever you store can go into any tank right away.
  • Want a clean start? Store everything in This tank moves every plant and decoration into storage in one go. Your animals stay put.
  • New in Supplies: an automatic feeder, so your fish never go hungry. They still love being fed by hand, and it makes them a little happier.
  • Also new: an auto water changer that keeps the water from dropping below Clean. Pristine water and scrubbing the glass are still up to you.
  • Plant orders now take the night into account, since plants barely grow with the lights off. If a plant order can’t be filled before the jobs change over, Jessie swaps it for one that can.
  • Plant jobs count plants you buy, so placing one back from storage doesn’t finish them by accident.

Polish and fixes

Version 0.7.2 · November 4, 2026

  • Fixed Jessie’s Jobs not changing over to a new day for some players. Yesterday’s leftover jobs are replaced too.
  • Plant orders only ask for plants that are big enough to trim today, and the job says when your plant will be ready.
  • The heater can be switched off in This tank, for cool-water fish like white clouds. If animals are too warm, Jessie says so now instead of saying they’re cold.
  • New in the Shop: an Emergency rescue kit, for a tank in real trouble. Like a real fish keeper would, it swaps out almost all the water at once, scrubs the glass and feeds everyone, even if Clean isn’t ready yet.
  • Tapping a notification about one of your tanks opens the game on that tank.
  • When an animal is unwell, the quick check under the treasure chest names the real problem (like dirty water) before any treats it would like.
  • New players no longer see pink “new” dots on everything in the Shop. The dots are for things added after you started playing.
  • Achievement cards stay up for a moment, so a tap you were already making doesn’t clear them before you can read them.
  • The launch screen stays up until your tank is ready, instead of a blank screen while the game catches up.
  • The Tanks page has clearer sections, so Storage is easier to find. When you can afford several tank upgrades, they’re all listed, and the upgrade buttons are a calmer teal.

Jessie’s Jobs and real daylight

Version 0.7.0 · November 1, 2026

  • Daylight follows the real sun: your rooms get dark at sunset where you are, and the tank glows under its lights until 10pm.
  • Two new rooms: a city apartment that lights up after dark, and a log cabin with a fire that’s lit in the evening.
  • Snow! From December through March, rainy days snow instead, in every room with a window.

  • A fish hotel, a tiki bar and a cocktail glass. [Credit: Julie Kamperides, Niki Kamperides]
  • A little red armchair, with arms for shrimp to sit on. [Credit: Niki Kamperides]
  • Bubble tint: colored or rainbow bubbles for your air stones. Pick the color from each air stone’s card. [Credit: Julie Kamperides]
  • No two the same: plants, rocks and wood each get their own size and direction. [Credit: Niki Kamperides]
  • Gravel favorites are spread around, so different animals love different gravel instead of everyone wanting dark sand. [Credit: Niki Kamperides]
  • Tanks are in the Shop now, and when you can afford a bigger tank, the Tanks tab says so loud and clear.
  • Two new rooms means Redecorator is a goal again if you’d already unlocked every room. You get the reward again when you finish.

Personalities and new friends

Version 0.6.0 · October 29, 2026

  • Every betta has a personality now: Hunter, Lazy lounger, Diva, Explorer or Shy. It changes how they swim, eat and what makes them happy. Tap a betta to meet yours.
  • African dwarf frogs in olive, brown and gray-green, with a rare golden one to find. They walk the bottom, kick up to the surface for air and sometimes just float there.
  • Thai micro crabs, one of the smallest crabs in the world, and safe with most bettas.
  • Malaysian trumpet snails in tan, dark brown and banded, with a rare gold. They hide in the gravel by day and come out at night.
  • Two new treats: live brine shrimp for fish to chase, and algae wafers for everyone on the bottom.
  • A Glowing sign: bigger than the wooden one, in a classic, oval or Vegas-style diamond shape, with marquee bulbs chasing around the edge and letters that light up one by one in the color you pick. [Credit: Julie Kamperides]
  • Signs can hang as high or low as you like: tap Move, then drag the sign up or down.
  • Four new cats to adopt: a black cat, a calico, a Scottish Fold with folded ears, and a spotted Bengal, a second Legendary.
  • Your first cat is your choice now: pick up Cat supplies in the Shop, then adopt any cat you like from the Cats tab.
  • Name your cats! Tap Name next to a cat in the Cats tab. With name tags on, a named cat’s name pops up when it comes to the glass. [Credit: Niki Kamperides]
  • Crazy cat lady now means all eleven cats. If you’d already earned it, it’s back on your list, and you get the reward again when you finish the set.
  • New colors: lavender, copper and black orchid bettas, and snowball and chocolate shrimp. Rare colors turn up a little more often. [Credit: Niki Kamperides]
  • Anything new in this version has a pink dot in the Shop, on its section and on the Shop button, until you take a look.
  • Earning an achievement is a proper moment now: an Achievement unlocked! card with confetti. Tap it to see the achievement in your list.
  • When Jessie finishes walking you through what’s new, the menu she opened closes too.
  • Peckish fish can be fed right away. Their card says so, and the countdown shows when they’ll be peckish next. [Credit: Julie Kamperides]
