Privacy Policy

Finside: Idle Aquarium · Last updated October 23, 2026

HomePrivacySupport

Short version: Finside doesn’t collect any information about you.

What Finside collects

Nothing. Finside has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

Where your game is saved

Your tanks are saved only on your own device. If you delete the app, the save is deleted with it. The game can make a backup code that you can copy; that code stays wherever you choose to put it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders like “your fish are hungry” are scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Finside doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

App stores and testing

Apple (App Store and TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) run their own stores and may collect information under their own privacy policies, such as crash reports or tester feedback you choose to send. Finside’s developer only sees what those services share with developers, like anonymous crash reports and feedback you submit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
