Privacy Policy

Finside: Idle Aquarium · Last updated November 9, 2026

HomeWhat’s NewSupportTermsPrivacy

Short version: Finside has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. If you use friend features, a server keeps a random player ID, your friend code, your friend list, and any postcards you choose to send. Nothing else, and you can delete it any time.

What Finside collects

If you never open the Friends tab: nothing. Your game stays on your device and Finside doesn’t connect to the internet.

If you use friend features, Finside’s server keeps:

  • a random player ID, made by the game the first time you open Friends. It isn’t linked to your name, email, phone number, contacts or any other app.
  • your friend code.
  • who your friends are, friend requests you’ve sent or received, and anyone you’ve blocked.
  • the names you give your friends. Only you can see them.
  • when you last opened the Friends tab.
  • postcards you choose to send, only when you tap Send: a description of that tank (its size, which animals, plants and decorations are in it and where, and the words on any signs) and the names in it (the tank’s and your animals’, cats’ and memorial stones’ names). Postcards aren’t photos: each friend’s game draws the picture itself. Names and sign words go through a word filter first, and anything that fails is left out. Only the friends you send a postcard to can see it, and each player’s Mailbox keeps their 50 newest postcards.
  • which postcards you’ve opened, so new ones can be marked.
  • which friends asked you for a postcard, and when.
  • if you report a postcard: who reported it, whose it was, and a copy of it, so it can be reviewed.

Finside doesn’t collect your name, email address, contacts, location or advertising ID, and nothing from your tanks unless you send a postcard. Your coins, progress and the rest of your save never leave your device.

Where it’s kept

Friend features run on Supabase, a database service, in the United States (Oregon). Like any website, the server sees your internet (IP) address when the game connects, and Supabase may keep short-term technical logs to run and protect the service. Finside doesn’t use your IP address for anything else.

How it’s used

Only to make friend features work: showing your friend code, your friends, your requests and postcards, and reviewing reported postcards. It isn’t sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting your online data

In the game: Friends → Delete my online data. That removes your player ID, friend code, friends, requests, blocks, the names you gave your friends, your postcards and asks, and reports about your postcards from the server. Your tanks stay on your device, untouched. You can also email to ask for it to be deleted.

If you don’t delete it, it’s kept while friend features exist. If friend features end, it’s deleted.

Where your game is saved

Your tanks are saved only on your own device. If you delete the app, the save is deleted with it. The game can make a backup code that you can copy; that code stays wherever you choose to put it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders like “your fish are hungry” are scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

If you contact the developer

If you email Finside, your email address and whatever you write are used only to reply to you and to fix or improve the game. They aren’t shared or sold, and you can ask for your messages to be deleted.

Beta testing

If you join a test version through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple shares the name and email address you were invited with, and any feedback, screenshots or crash reports you choose to send, with the developer. They’re used only to run the test and fix problems.

Children

Friend features don’t ask anyone for personal information, and players never see each other’s names: only friend codes, and names each player gives their own friends privately.

App stores and testing

Apple (App Store and TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) run their own stores and may collect information under their own privacy policies, such as crash reports or tester feedback you choose to send. Finside’s developer only sees what those services share with developers, like anonymous crash reports and feedback you submit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
