Finside: Idle Aquarium

A cozy, true-to-life idle aquarium game.

HomeWhat’s NewSupportTermsPrivacy

Start with a 1 gallon tank on your counter. Cycle the water, plant some java moss, and add your first cherry shrimp. Every plant and animal is a real species that behaves like the real thing: bettas eat shrimp, shy fish need plants to hide in, schooling fish want a group, and new water has to cycle before anything can live in it.

Your tanks keep living while you’re away. Babies hatch, plants grow, and coins pile up in the treasure chest, so check in a few times a day. Hunt for rare colors, fill your Aquadex, raise multiple tanks, and adopt a cat or two to watch the fish.

What’s in the tank

  • 24 real species of fish, shrimp and snails, and 13 real plants
  • Tanks from 1 to 20 gallons, with room for up to 8 at once
  • Breeding and rare color forms, 9 betta colors, and color varieties of shrimp and snails
  • Animals with moods: each one shows what it likes and what would make it happier, and a risk meter warns you before you mix species that don’t get along
  • Real care: cycling, water changes, algae, illness and medicine, trimming plants, and CO2 for a planted tank
  • Caves, hammocks, driftwood and ledges your animals actually use, and plants that grow on wood and rock
  • Day and night that follow your clock, rooms with weather, and cats that wander over to watch the fish
  • On the phone apps, notifications when fish get hungry or babies hatch
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
