Finside

An idle aquarium game. Cozy, and true to life.

HomePrivacySupport

Start with a 1 gallon tank on your counter. Cycle the water, plant some java moss, add your first cherry shrimp, and grow it into a row of bigger tanks. Every plant and animal is a real species that behaves like the real thing.

Your tanks keep living while you’re away: babies hatch, plants grow and coins pile up in the treasure chest.
